# Introduction to Derivatives

**Derivatives** are contracts / agreements that derives its value from the underlying. The underlying can be assets (commodity, metal, crude, currency, shares, bonds, CDS, index funds) or any variables (weather - in case of sport events)

Derivatives are broadly classified into following categories 

- Forward Contracts
- Futures
- Options
- Swaps 

Derivatives are not investments they can be considered as risk assessment tool to hedge our portfolio.
